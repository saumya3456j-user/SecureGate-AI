📱 Mobile SMS Gateways

SecureGate AI automatically sends OTP via SMS to registered mobile numbers. Supports Fast2SMS (India) and Twilio (International). Configure in backend/.env: 

# India Mobile SMS (Fast2SMS)
FAST2SMS_API_KEY=your_key_here

# International SMS (Twilio)
TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID=your_sid
TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN=your_token
TWILIO_PHONE_NUMBER=+1234567890
